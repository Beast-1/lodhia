# Lohia Legacy Design

Design and build an exceptionally premium, visually striking landing page for **Lohia Ayurvedic Pharmacy**, an established Ayurvedic pharmacy in Guwahati, India, serving customers since **1988**. The website should feel like a modern digital flagship for a respected Ayurvedic institution: rich botanical, editorial, sophisticated, warm, authentic and distinctly Indian, combining contemporary luxury wellness design with the visual richness of Ayurvedic plants, herbs, roots and traditional knowledge. Avoid the typical AI-generated website aesthetic completely: no purple/blue gradients, glassmorphism, floating blobs, excessive rounded cards, generic SaaS layouts, excessive shadows, random 3D graphics or cookie-cutter feature sections. Use a refined palette of deep botanical green, warm ivory/cream, turmeric yellow, muted terracotta, earthy brown and restrained antique gold. Use elegant editorial serif typography paired with a clean modern sans-serif, oversized headlines, strong visual hierarchy, generous whitespace, asymmetric compositions, subtle borders and sophisticated micro-interactions. The hero should be cinematic and image-led, featuring beautiful Ayurvedic botanical imagery with the headline **“Ancient wisdom. Rooted in trust since 1988.”** and a clear CTA such as **“Explore Ayurveda”** or **“Discover Lohia.”** Follow the hero with a visually rich heritage/story section introducing Lohia and its 1988 legacy, a premium preview of Ayurvedic medicines and wellness products, and a distinctive **“Ayurvedic Library”** section introducing future content about plants, herbs and ingredients, including their traditional uses, benefits, preparations and imagery. Preview this library visually with beautiful botanical compositions rather than boring cards. Add a section introducing the physical pharmacy in Guwahati with space for real storefront photography, location and WhatsApp/contact CTA, followed by trust/reputation content and a refined footer. The landing page should feel immersive and editorial, using real photography placeholders where appropriate and making photography, typography and composition more important than UI decoration. Design it mobile-first and fully responsive. Use subtle scroll reveals, image transitions and elegant hover interactions, but keep animations restrained and premium. Build the page as a clean, reusable React/TypeScript/Tailwind project with well-structured components so later pages can expand into **Pharmacy, Product Catalogue, Ayurvedic Library, Plants & Herbs, Ingredients, Articles, Our Story, Visit Us and Online Ordering**. The final result should look like a premium agency-designed website, not an AI-generated template.
